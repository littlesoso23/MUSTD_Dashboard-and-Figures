Figure 4
Monthly expiry trend
Raw expired count per month (bars), the naive expiry rate against that month's Dashboard entrants (slate line), and a cohort-adjusted rate that accounts for the ~3-week BL window (dashed plum line). Because the window spans a month boundary, most people expiring in a given month actually entered the month before.
Calculations
Naive rate = expired ÷ Dashboard entrants that month Cohort-adjusted rate = expired ÷ (0.7 × prior-month Dashboard + 0.3 × this-month Dashboard)
Why the adjustment: with a ~21-day window, only entrants from the first ~9 days of a month can expire within that same month, so roughly 70% of a cohort's expirations land in the following calendar month (assuming uniform within-month entry). The naive rate divides one cohort's expirations by a different cohort's size.
Adjusted: Mar 32 ÷ 114 = 28% · Apr 43 ÷ 106 = 41% · May 41 ÷ 137 = 30% · Jun 20 ÷ 145 = 14%. (Feb omitted — no prior-month cohort in the data.)
Censoring caveat: the latest month is right-censored under either rate — most of its entrants are still inside their window, so its expiry count will rise as windows close. The same lag applies (more mildly) to conversion: a month's completions partly come from the prior month's entrants.
